I am a Master of Law candidate at China University of Political Science and Law (CUPL), where I also earned my LL.B. I have previously served as a research assistant at Tsinghua University and Alibaba Group, and studied as a exchange student at Fordham University School of Law. I am applying to PhD programs for Fall 2027 admission.

My research lies at the intersection of law, communication, and computer science, focusing on technology governance, AI evaluation, and information privacy. I am formally trained in doctrinal legal research — reading statutes, case law, and legislative history closely in both Chinese and English — and I hold the PRC legal professional qualification. More recently, I have been working with computational text analysis, using large language models to study legal and social media texts at a scale that close reading alone cannot reach.

I am always glad to hear from people working on AI governance, legal NLP, or privacy. The fastest way to reach me is by email.
